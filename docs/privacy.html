Privacy Policy - The Hole Pro

Last updated: 14 November 2026

"The Hole Pro" ("the app") is a free mobile game. This policy explains what information the app and its partners handle.

Information we collect

The game works offline. The only network use is advertising and purchases (below).

We do not ask you to create an account and we do not collect your name, email, phone number or location.

The app stores your game progress (coins, level, settings, purchases) only on your device.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads using Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information and ad interaction data to show and measure ads, and to prevent fraud. Where required (EEA, UK), the app asks for your consent before personalised ads. You can reset or opt out of personalised ads in your device settings (Settings > Google > Ads) and change your consent choice in the app where offered.

Learn more: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

In-app purchases (Google Play)

Purchases are processed by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment details. Google Play tells the app which items you own so they can be unlocked.

Children

The app is intended for players aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has used the app, contact us and we will help.

Data sharing and security

We do not sell personal data. Data handled by Google (ads, purchases) is transmitted securely and governed by Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Your choices

You can uninstall the app at any time to remove its locally stored data, and manage ad personalisation in your Android settings.

Changes

We may update this policy; the latest version will be posted at this URL.

Contact

Open an issue at https://github.com/mrcarkstudios-lang/the-hole-pro/issues (public) and we will respond. Please do not post personal information in a public issue; ask for a private way to contact us instead.
